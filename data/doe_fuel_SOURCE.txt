Source of data/doe_fuel_prices_2026-10-20.csv

Department of Energy (Philippines), Price Monitoring of Liquid Fuels, week of 20-26 October 2026.
Reports obtained from the DOE retail pump price pages (https://doe.gov.ph/retail-pump-prices-metro-manila and the regional pages linked from it):

  NCR_price_monitoring_2026-10-20          NCR_Price_Monitoring_10202026.pdf (Oil Industry Management Bureau)
  NORTH_LUZON_price_monitoring_2026-10-20  NORTHERN_LUZON_LIQUID_FUEL_PRICE_MONITORING_REPORT_FOR_THE_PERIOD_OF_OCT_20-26_2026.pdf (Luzon Field Office; CAR, Regions I-III)
  REGION_IV-A_price_monitoring_2026-10-20  Region_IV-A_CALABARZON.pdf (Oil Industry Management Bureau)
  REGION_IV-B_price_monitoring_2026-10-20  Region_IV-B_MIMAROPA.pdf (Oil Industry Management Bureau)
  REGION_V_price_monitoring_2026-10-20     Region_V_Bicol.pdf (Oil Industry Management Bureau)
  VISAYAS_price_monitoring_2026-10-20      NEW_VFO_PRICE_MONITORING_102026 (with LGU and DOE Field).pdf (Visayas Field Office; Regions VI-VIII and NIR)
  MINDANAO_price_monitoring_2026-10-20     42. LFRO-Price-Monitoring-October-20-26-2026.pdf (Mindanao Field Office; Regions IX-XIII and BARMM)

The PDFs are not redistributed. To rebuild the CSV, convert each PDF with
`pdftotext -layout` into data/doe_fuel/<name>.txt using the names above, then run
`python data/extract_doe_fuel.py`.

Each row is one product at one monitored city or municipality. price_min/max are
recomputed from the brand columns (every price between the product name and the
OVERALL RANGE column); doe_overall_range_min/max are the report's own OVERALL RANGE.
They differ in 16 rows (overall_range_differs = True), where the OVERALL RANGE column
leaves out a brand column or, at Alicia (Isabela, Region II), is shifted up by one
row; these were checked against the PDF pages. Rows with no prices are omitted.

Two prices are flagged as outliers but kept, because the reports print them in both
the brand column and the overall range: diesel PHP 55.80/L in Bangued City, Abra
(CAR), and RON 97 PHP 158.30/L at Petron, Ozamiz City (Region X), which DOE's own
Mindanao summary page also lists as the top of its RON 97/100 range.
